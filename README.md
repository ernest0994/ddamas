# DDamas

Damas dominicanas. The pieces are bottle caps.

A ficha wears the flag on the outside. A dama is that same cap turned over: bare metal, flag on the liner. Ten flags. 10×10, the way the table plays it.

- A man walks forward, one diagonal step.
- A man captures forward and backward. A capture is mandatory, and the longest one wins.
- Captured caps stay until the chain ends.
- A man becomes a dama only when it stops on the last rank. The dama flies.

Play against the machine, or with two people on one phone.
